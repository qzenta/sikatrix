South African businesses deal with a steady stream of tax and compliance deadlines. Miss one and you pay penalties and interest. Stay ahead of them and your cash flow stays predictable and your records stay clean.

This calendar covers the 2026/2027 tax year, which runs from 1 March 2026 to 28 February 2027. It includes monthly EMP201 payroll submissions, VAT returns, provisional tax, the 2026 filing season and the employer reconciliations.

Where a SARS deadline falls on a weekend or public holiday, SARS moves it to the last business day before that date. The dates below already include that adjustment.

## Understanding the SA Tax Year

**Individuals, sole proprietors and trusts:** 1 March 2026 to 28 February 2027.

**Companies and close corporations:** Your tax year follows your financial year-end. The provisional tax dates below assume a February year-end. If your year-end is in another month, your provisional tax and ITR14 dates move with it.

---

## Monthly Recurring Deadlines

### EMP201: PAYE, UIF and SDL

If you have employees, you submit your EMP201 and pay PAYE, UIF and SDL by the 7th of the following month. If the 7th is a weekend or public holiday, the deadline is the last business day before it.

SDL applies once your total payroll is expected to exceed R500,000 over the next 12 months. UIF is 1% from the employee and 1% from the employer. Our guide to [PAYE, UIF and SDL](/resources/paye-uif-sdl-explained) explains how each one works.

### VAT201: VAT returns

If you file and pay on eFiling, the VAT201 and payment are due by the last business day of the month after your tax period ends. Manual returns are due by the 25th. Most SMEs are on two-monthly periods: Category A periods end in odd months (January, March, May and so on) and Category B periods end in even months.

## The 2026/2027 Month-by-Month Calendar

### March 2026: New tax year begins

- 6 March: EMP201 for February 2026 payroll (7 March is a Saturday)
- Category B vendors: VAT201 for January to February 2026 due by 31 March on eFiling
- Start of the 2026/2027 tax year for individuals and February year-end companies

### April 2026

- 7 April: EMP201 for March 2026 payroll
- 1 April: EMP501 annual reconciliation window opens for the 2025/2026 payroll year
- 1 April: Compensation Fund Return of Earnings window opens

### May 2026

- 7 May: EMP201 for April 2026 payroll
- 31 May: EMP501 annual reconciliation window closes (1 March 2025 to 28 February 2026). 31 May 2026 is a Sunday, so submit by Friday 29 May.

### June 2026

- 5 June: EMP201 for May 2026 payroll (7 June is a Sunday)
- 30 June: COIDA Return of Earnings closes, as gazetted in Notice No. 3894 of 2026

### July 2026: Filing season opens

- 7 July: EMP201 for June 2026 payroll
- 1 to 12 July: SARS auto-assessments
- 13 July: Filing opens for non-provisional individuals and provisional taxpayers

### August 2026: First provisional tax payment

- 7 August: EMP201 for July 2026 payroll
- 31 August: First provisional tax payment (IRP6) for the 2026/2027 year

### September 2026

- 7 September: EMP201 for August 2026 payroll
- 19 September: Trust filing season opens
- 21 September: EMP501 interim reconciliation window opens (1 March to 31 August 2026)

### October 2026

- 7 October: EMP201 for September 2026 payroll
- 23 October: Filing deadline for non-provisional individuals
- 31 October: EMP501 interim reconciliation window closes. 31 October 2026 is a Saturday, so do not leave it to the last day.

### November 2026

- 6 November: EMP201 for October 2026 payroll (7 November is a Saturday)
- Use the quieter month to update your full-year income estimate for the February payment

### December 2026

- 7 December: EMP201 for November 2026 payroll
- Factor December trading into your second provisional tax estimate, and submit payroll early before the holiday period

### January 2027

- 7 January: EMP201 for December 2026 payroll
- 22 January: Filing deadline for provisional taxpayers and trusts (2026 returns)

### February 2027: Second provisional tax payment and year-end

- 5 February: EMP201 for January 2027 payroll (7 February is a Sunday)
- 26 February: Second provisional tax payment (IRP6). The nominal date, 28 February 2027, is a Sunday.
- 28 February: End of the 2026/2027 tax year

Our [provisional tax guide](/resources/sars-provisional-tax-guide-2025) explains how the two payments are calculated.

## Additional Annual Obligations

### CIPC annual returns

Companies file an annual return with CIPC within 30 business days after the anniversary of their incorporation date. The date is different for every company, so check yours and set a reminder well ahead.

### Annual financial statements

Your annual financial statements follow your financial year-end. File the ITR14 within 12 months of year-end, but there is no reason to wait that long once the statements are done.

### Third provisional tax payment (voluntary)

Individuals and February year-end companies can make a voluntary top-up by the last business day of September 2027 (30 September 2027). It reduces the interest SARS charges if your earlier estimates fell short.

### Looking ahead to 2027

The 2027 EMP501 annual reconciliation follows SARS's standing window of 1 April to 31 May. SARS confirms each year's dates in its annual notice, so check them in April 2027.

## Five Habits That Keep SMEs Compliant All Year

**1. Use cloud accounting.** Real-time data in QuickBooks, Xero or Sage makes provisional tax estimates and VAT returns faster and more accurate.

**2. Reconcile monthly.** Monthly bank reconciliations catch errors before they compound.

**3. Pay SARS on time.** Late payment penalties and interest cost more than the short-term cash saving.

**4. Separate business and personal banking.** Mixed accounts make reconciliations harder and put deductions at risk.

**5. Build a tax provision.** Set aside a share of revenue each month for SARS so the February payment is not a shock.
