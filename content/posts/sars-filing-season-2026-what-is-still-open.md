Filing season 2026 is now in its final stretch. The deadline for non-provisional individuals passed on 23 October 2026, but two groups still have time: provisional taxpayers and trusts. If you are in either group, or you missed the October deadline, this is what you need to know.

## How filing season 2026 ran

SARS published the dates for the 2026 year of assessment (1 March 2025 to 28 February 2026) in Notice 7422, Government Gazette 54598, on 30 April 2026. The season ran in three stages.

**Auto-assessments:** 1 to 12 July 2026. SARS issued assessments based on the information it already held from employers, medical schemes, retirement funds and banks.

**Non-provisional individuals:** 13 July to 23 October 2026. This window is now closed.

**Provisional taxpayers:** 13 July 2026 to 22 January 2027.

**Trusts:** 19 September 2026 to 22 January 2027.

If you want the background on how each stage works, our [2026 filing season guide](/resources/sars-tax-season-2026-filing-guide) and our article on [auto-assessments](/resources/auto-assessment-2026-south-africa) cover them in detail.

## Who did not need to file

Notice 7422 sets out when a natural person is not required to submit a return. The best-known rule covers employees with one employer. You did not need to file if your gross income for the 2026 year of assessment consisted only of remuneration from a single employer that did not exceed R500,000, with employees' tax deducted or withheld in terms of the SARS deduction tables.

The notice also allows a few other types of income alongside that salary without triggering a return. These include South African interest (other than from a tax-free investment) of not more than R23,800 if you were under 65, or R34,500 if you were 65 or older, and amounts from a tax-free investment.

The exemption does not apply if any of the following were true for you during the year:

- You received a travel allowance, other than a reimbursement at or below the simplified rate per kilometre
- You had the use of an employer-provided vehicle, a taxable benefit under paragraph 7 of the Seventh Schedule
- You earned any amount for services rendered outside South Africa

Separately, if SARS notified you in writing that you were eligible for auto-assessment, you did not need to file as long as the information in its records was complete and correct.

Being below the threshold does not stop you from filing. If you are owed a refund because of medical expenses, retirement annuity contributions or a second source of income, filing a return is how you claim it. SARS's own guidance is simple: if you are uncertain whether you should file, file.

## What is still open: provisional taxpayers and trusts

You are a provisional taxpayer if you earn income other than remuneration, such as business, rental or freelance income. Your 2026 return is due by 22 January 2027. The same date applies to trusts.

The January deadline falls in the slowest part of the year for most practices, and the December break eats into the time you have. Start now. A provisional return usually needs more than an IRP5:

- Income statements for your business or rental property for 1 March 2025 to 28 February 2026
- Supporting records for every expense you claim
- Your IRP6 provisional tax statements for the year
- Medical, retirement annuity and investment certificates
- For trusts, the trust deed, distribution records and beneficiary details

Filing early also tells you sooner whether you owe SARS money. That matters, because your second provisional tax payment for the 2026/2027 year is due at the end of February 2027. Our article on [the second provisional tax period](/resources/provisional-tax-second-period-2027-sme) explains how to prepare for it.

## If you missed 23 October

If you were a non-provisional taxpayer who had to file and did not, your return is now late. SARS can impose administrative non-compliance penalties for outstanding returns, and they continue for every month a return stays outstanding. The answer is not to wait. File as soon as you can, check your eFiling profile for any penalty notices, and keep proof of submission.

If you believe you were not required to file under Notice 7422, check the conditions above carefully. The travel allowance, company car and foreign service exclusions catch many salaried people by surprise.

## Your next steps

- Check whether you are a provisional taxpayer or a trustee with a January deadline
- Gather your income records and certificates now, before the December break
- If you missed the October deadline, file without further delay
- Diarise 22 January 2027 and the end of February 2027 for your next provisional tax payment
